1. Introduction & Purpose

This Privacy Policy applies strictly to the official mobile application "Sri Sri Radha Krishna Chandra - Admin Duty Terminal" (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), owned and operated by Sri Sri Radha Krishna Chandra Mandir (ISKCON Hadapsar, Pune, Maharashtra, India).

The Application is an enterprise administrative and duty tracking tool designed exclusively for authorized temple administrators, staff, and field duty volunteers. It is not intended for general commercial public use. We are committed to protecting the privacy, dignity, and personal safety of our administrative officers in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India) and Google Play Developer Policies.

⚠️ 2. Prominent Disclosure: Real-Time Location & Foreground Service Access

The Application collects, accesses, monitors, and transmits precise real-time Location Data (GPS and Network-based coordinates) even when the Application is running in the background, minimized, or when the screen is locked, through an active Android Foreground Service.

  • Exact Data Collected: Latitude, Longitude, Altitude, Accuracy (in meters), Travel Speed (km/h), and Bearing.
  • Why Location is Collected: To enable real-time field duty accountability, geofenced temple administrative coordination, movement verification, and immediate 1-click rescue navigation in case of an emergency or distress (SOS).
  • When Location is Collected: Continuous tracking is activated strictly upon authorized login and remains active during official duty shifts. A persistent, non-dismissible notification in the Android notification drawer clearly notifies the user that duty location monitoring is active.
  • No Advertising: Location data is NEVER used for advertising, behavioral marketing, cross-app tracking, or sold to any third-party brokers.

3. Hardware Identification & Device Binding Policy

To enforce our strict "One Device = One Account" security policy and prevent credential theft or unauthorized impersonation, the Application collects:

  • Hardware Fingerprint: A one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash derived from the device's hardware parameters (Device Model, Manufacturer, Board, and Android ID).
  • Device Telemetry: Battery percentage, battery charging state, and operating system build parameters.
  • Tamper Detection: Notification signals when device Location (GPS) hardware toggles are altered during an active duty shift.

4. User Information Collected

We process the following administrative credentials provided during onboarding:

  • Official Full Name
  • Assigned Institutional Email Address
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Audit logs of administrative module access within the portal

5. Data Storage & Third-Party Service Providers

All location heartbeats, hardware registries, and administrative audit logs are transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 HTTPS encryption to the following enterprise-grade infrastructure:

Google Firebase (Google Cloud) Authentication & Realtime Database (Singapore Region: asia-southeast1). Complies with ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3, and GDPR.
Vercel Inc. Secure hosting environment for the administrative web interface and encrypted dashboard communication.

6. Emergency Safety & Law Enforcement Disclosure

We do not share personal data with external entities except in the following critical circumstances:

  • Emergency Distress (SOS): If an officer triggers an emergency alert or is reported missing, unresponsive, or in immediate physical danger, precise GPS coordinates and movement breadcrumbs are formatted into official dispatch dossiers to be shared directly with local police authorities (e.g., Pune Police Control Room) and registered emergency contacts for immediate rescue navigation.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with legitimate legal orders, warrants, or court summons under Indian law (Section 69 of the Information Technology Act).

7. Data Retention, Access & Deletion Rights

Location breadcrumbs and movement trails are automatically cleared after a maximum of 90 days. Live radar pins only retain the latest valid GPS pulse.

Data Deletion Request: If an administrative officer resigns or discontinues temple duty services, they have the legal right to request the complete deletion of their account credentials, device binding hashes, and past location history. Requests can be submitted directly to our Data Protection Officer at the email listed below, and all related records will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

8. Information Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, including cryptographic hashing of hardware parameters, role-based database read/write rules, and end-to-end HTTPS/TLS data transport to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, or disclosure.
